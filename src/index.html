Nyquist Theorem: Maximum data rate without noise

Formula: 2 × B × log₂(L)

Shannon Theorem: Maximum data rate with noise

Formula: B × log₂(1 + S/N)

SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio): Determines channel quality

Interpretation:

  • Low SNR → Poor channel → Simple modulation (BPSK)
  • Medium SNR → Moderate channel → QPSK
  • High SNR → Good channel → QAM
